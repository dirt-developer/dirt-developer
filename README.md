To survive, it opens its dormant genetic database and unleashes its **Laccase and Peroxidase enzyme payloads** directly against the plastic's ultra-stable carbon-carbon single bonds. It begins chewing through the plastic simply because it is the only carbon fuel left on the drive.

### 🌊 Phase 03: The Nano-Plastic Ghost Purge (Days 61–120)
